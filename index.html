背景The problem

每天产生的废塑料、废轮胎里，
藏着大量尚未得到充分利用的碳。 Every day, waste plastics and tyres hold carbon that is still largely unused.

看看如何转化See how we convert it

低值废塑料、废轮胎与混合有机固废持续产生，填埋、焚烧和物理回收都难以覆盖。把其中的碳转化为航空燃料的原料，是我们要解决的问题。

Low-value plastics, tyres and mixed organic waste keep coming, and landfill, incineration and mechanical recycling can’t absorb them. Turning that carbon into feedstock for aviation fuel is the problem we work on.

为什么是现在Why now

SAF 强制使用要求，正在形成市场需求。 SAF mandates arecreating market demand.

SAF 强制使用要求正在将航空减碳目标转化为市场需求。扩大生产，需要更多原料来源与配套转化技术。InnoHelix 聚焦其中的催化升级环节，为废弃碳进入航空燃料生产链开发关键技术。

SAF mandates are turning aviation’s decarbonisation targets into market demand. Scaling production needs more feedstock and the conversion technology to go with it. InnoHelix focuses on the catalytic upgrading step, building the technology that lets waste carbon enter the aviation-fuel supply chain.

我们的技术Our technology

以催化升级模块，连接固废热解与航油合成。 A catalytic upgrading modulebetween waste pyrolysis and jet-fuel synthesis.

上游将固废热解为复杂气体，下游利用合成气生产航空燃料。InnoHelix 开发两者之间的催化升级接口，将复杂热解气转化为合成气，衔接上下游工艺。

Upstream, waste is pyrolysed into a complex gas. Downstream, syngas is made into aviation fuel. InnoHelix develops the catalytic upgrading interface between the two, converting complex pyrolysis gas into syngas so both processes connect.

固废侧Waste side

处置成本高，去向受限。Costly to dispose of, nowhere to go.

低值废塑料、复合包装、废旧纺织物与混合有机固废持续产生，组分复杂、污染度高。填埋、焚烧发电和物理回收都无法覆盖低值料，其中的碳资源没有被回收。

Low-value plastics, composite packaging, textiles and mixed organic waste keep coming — complex and contaminated. Landfill, incineration and mechanical recycling can't handle low-grade material, and its carbon goes unrecovered.

航空侧Aviation side

必须减碳，但 SAF 供给不足。It must decarbonise — but SAF is scarce.

商用大飞机完全依赖液体燃料，SAF 是唯一合规的替代液体燃料。当前 SAF 占航油不足 1%，现有 HEFA 路线受废弃油脂供给上限约束。

Commercial jets run on liquid fuel, and SAF is the only compliant drop-in alternative. Today SAF is under 1% of jet fuel, and the dominant HEFA route is capped by the supply of waste oils.

两级催化重整Two-stage catalytic reforming

先保护，再深度转化。Protect first. Then convert deeply.

独特的两级催化反应结构：第一段牺牲性床层承接焦油与多环芳烃，第二段主重整床专注深度转化——提升抗中毒性与转化效率。

A two-stage structure: a sacrificial first bed takes the tar and polyaromatics so the main reforming bed can focus on deep conversion — better poisoning resistance, higher efficiency.
